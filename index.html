02 / The author

To my dearest reader:

Whenever someone asks me what I write about, I struggle to find an answer.

Sometimes, I just like the way certain words weave together. Sometimes, I want to create something that my future grandchildren would appreciate. Sometimes, I move my pen simply because I don't know who I'd be if I stopped.

I hope, through all my moods and phases, I've managed to create something you can appreciate.

-CT
